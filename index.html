Navid Salahian

Machine learning researcher, University of Kurdistan

I am a machine learning researcher at the University of Kurdistan and a member of the Algebraic Machine Learning team led by Dr. Fardin Akhlaghian. My research focuses on representation learning from unlabeled and partially labeled data, with an emphasis on nonnegative matrix factorization and deep learning, including self-supervised learning and adversarial training. I apply these methods to data clustering, matrix completion, multi-view multi-label feature selection, and robust data representation. Alongside my research, I have over ten years of experience in Python programming and software development.
